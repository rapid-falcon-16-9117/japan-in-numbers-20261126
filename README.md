Japan in Numbers — the national statistics of Japan in one place: 9 pages, 14 charts and 91 sourced figures covering population, economy, work, prices, health, education and regions. Every figure names its source and year on the page where it appears (Statistics Bureau of Japan, Cabinet Office, MHLW, Ministry of Finance, IMF, World Bank, OECD and the UN). The site is plain static files with no build step and no network request: it opens in desktop Chrome and on a phone, works offline, and every control does what it says — a live indicator search, a GDP measure selector, a CPI sort toggle, a life-expectancy sex toggle, and a sortable, filterable prefecture table.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
